02 · Duplicates

Merging duplicates is easy. Not merging the wrong people is the job.

Rules: same personal email; or same phone and names that agree (nicknames, one typo); or same company and names that agree. A shared switchboard number alone never counts, and two different personal emails send a group to a person for review instead of merging. Scored pair by pair against the 180 duplicate groups planted in the leads.

Where it is wrong, and what happens then. The 9 wrong pairs are namesakes at one company sharing a switchboard: different emails, so all of them went to review and none were merged. Of 217 groups, 177 merged automatically (0 wrong) and 40 wait for a person (31 real, 9 not). The 14 missed pairs are people who came back with a new personal email and no phone: nothing in the record links them. Contacts: 100% / 100%.

Leads that are already contacts

108 leads match an existing contact (105 correct of 110 planted). Zoho merges only inside one module, so these are listed for converting into the existing contact rather than merged.

How merges run

Zoho's Merge Records API takes one master and up to two others per call, so 177 groups took 177 calls. The kept record is the most complete one (oldest on a tie) and pulls in any field it is missing from the others. Merges can't be undone through the API, which is why the review queue exists.

04 · Workflows

Every edition caps workflow e-mails per user, per day

Zoho allows 100 workflow e-mails per user licence a day on Standard and 200 on Professional. The sample org's 16 rules were replayed against 30 days of activity (6 licences): two rules that mail on every edit, plus one rule copied twice, send most of the volume.

Settings checklist from the plan

Changes the API should not make on its own: removing picklist options, retiring fields (after export), switching rules off. 15 steps, each with where to find it.

  1. Fields Lead Source (Contacts, Leads, Deals): remove 14 merged variants and 7 options nobody uses, after the record updatesSetup > Modules and Fields > each module > Lead Source
  2. Pipeline Stage: remove the retired stages "Value Proposition", "Id. Decision Makers" from the picklist once their deals have movedSetup > Modules and Fields > Deals > Stage
  3. Fields Export then retire "Birthday Card" (13 records filled)Setup > Modules and Fields > Contacts
  4. Fields Export then retire "Fax (alt)" (10 records filled)Setup > Modules and Fields > Leads
  5. Fields Export then retire "Trade Show 2022" (45 records filled)Setup > Modules and Fields > Leads
  6. Fields Export then retire "Temp Import Batch" (0 records filled)Setup > Modules and Fields > Leads
  7. Fields Export then retire "Second Email (old)" (26 records filled)Setup > Modules and Fields > Leads
  8. Workflows Deactivate the copy: "New web lead - notify owner" = "New web lead - notify owner (copy)"Setup > Automation > Workflow Rules
  9. Workflows Replace "Lead changed - notify owner" (Leads), "Deal changed - notify owner" (Deals) with alerts on the changes that matter (new hot lead, stage change) and a daily digest reportSetup > Automation > Workflow Rules; Reports > Schedule
  10. Workflows Delete "Value Proposition stage task" (its stage is gone)Setup > Automation > Workflow Rules
  11. Workflows Confirm or remove "Push lead to old email tool": POST to mail-sync (retired 2024)Setup > Automation > Actions > Webhooks
  12. Workflows Delete inactive/never-run rules: "Trade show 2022 follow-up", "Test rule do not use", "Contact changed - notify owner", "Value Proposition stage task"Setup > Automation > Workflow Rules
  13. Duplicates Turn on duplicate check for Email on web forms and imports so new duplicates stop at the doorSetup > Modules and Fields > Leads > Email (unique) / Web Forms
  14. Leads Assignment rule for new leads (round robin by region) so no lead waits unownedSetup > Automation > Assignment Rules
  15. Pipeline Make Loss Reason mandatory when Stage = Closed Lost (layout rule or validation rule)Setup > Modules and Fields > Deals > Layout Rules

Read-only by default

  • Snapshot and audit need only READ scopes: users, fields, layouts, pipeline, workflow rules, modules
  • Paging switches to page_token past 2,000 records; field lists are split at 50
  • 43 calls / 43 credits to read all 5,776 records and settings

Changes are reversible where Zoho allows

  • Dry run lists every request and its credit cost before anything changes
  • Every update stores its before-value; zcrm rollback journal.json puts them back
  • Merges can't be reversed via the API, so groups with conflicting emails wait for approval

Checked

  • tests 17, pass 17, fail 0: matching rules, picklist grouping, credit maths, paging past 2,000, API limits, apply, rollback
  • Credit estimate 854 = credits the API charged 854
  • Second run after apply: 0 record changes left

Measured

  • Everything on this site comes from one run of the kit against a mock of the Zoho CRM v8 API that follows the documented endpoints, response shapes, paging rules, merge and update limits and credit costs.
  • Duplicate and picklist scores are counted against the problems planted in the sample org.
  • E-mail counts replay 30 days of sample activity through the rules' real triggers and criteria.

Not measured

  • Your org. The sample was written by the same person as the matching rules, so the scores show the rules work on these patterns, not a promise for your data; that is why every merge group is listed for approval first.
  • Live Zoho response times; real API latency will make runs slower than the mock's 12 s.
  • The activity log behind the e-mail chart is generated, so the shape is realistic but the exact volumes are modelled.
